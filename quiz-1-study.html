Two models, two jobs

Use the model that matches the question being asked.

Circular flow · who exchanges what?

Households provide factors of production to firms through factor markets; firms pay income. Firms sell goods and services to households through product markets; households pay firms. Money flows opposite to real resources, goods and services.

PPF · what is feasible, and what is given up?

Shows the maximum combinations of two outputs with current resources and technology. On = feasible and efficient; inside = feasible but below capacity; outside = currently unattainable.

Movement along the PPF

Same capacity, different output mix. A change in demand may change the chosen point, but does not itself change productive capacity.

Shift of the PPF

Capacity changes. More resources or productive technology can shift it outward; a loss of resources can shift it inward. A technology affecting just one output may shift one side more.

Positive vs normative

Positive claims describe something testable with evidence. Normative claims express what should happen and depend on values. Evidence informs values debates but cannot settle them alone.

Marginal decision rule

Compare the extra benefit with every extra cost: continue if MB ≥ MC. At equality, the action meets the course rule.
